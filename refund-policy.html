Refund Policy

Refunds will only be processed under the following circumstances:

  • Mentor is unavailable or misses a confirmed session
  • Technical issues that prevent the session from taking place
All refund requests must be submitted via email to hello@getyourguidance.com with session details. Refunds, if approved, will be processed within 7 working days.
No refunds will be issued for completed sessions or dissatisfaction with mentor guidance, unless there’s evidence of misconduct or violation of platform policies.
